Our Legacy & Capability

Over 30 Years of Engineering Leadership in Egypt

Founded in 1995 by Eng. Hemdan Sakr, Al Sakr Top Group (ALSAKR for Trading & Manufacturing) was established to equip Egyptian and regional manufacturers with world-class packaging and industrial handling equipment built with local engineering pride.

Operating from our manufacturing facility in Industrial Area C3, 10th of Ramadan City, our integrated joint-stock company combines heavy machinery fabrication, precision CNC machining, and automated controls engineering under one roof.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, hygienic, and highly productive packaging machinery and conveyor systems that eliminate downtime and elevate manufacturing capabilities across Egypt and the MENA region.

Our Vision

To stand as the most trusted single-source industrial engineering partner for turnkey packaging and material handling lines.
